1First, the floor

Choose your floor.

Pro and Light are basket weave floors: two layers of tubes, so each jump is spread over more pads for a more consistent feel. The basket weave is an ancient martial arts sprung floor design that became popular in the dance industry. Pro and Light use the same plywood and the same joints, survive a flood, and are designed to bend the same, within 1% by the math. Kit and Go are single layer floors, with one layer of tubes. They are similar in softness to Pro and a little stiffer than Light.

Pro

The best and most consistent for dancers.

A basket weave sprung floor with fiberglass tubes on a 16 in. span. Pro has the tightest grid and the most contact pads under the plywood, so it has the least reverberation: a dancer feels less movement from the people dancing nearby. Each impact is spread over more pads, for the most consistent spring.

We built Pro and Light floors side by side and tested them with professional dancers. 80% preferred Pro, and all of them could feel a subtle difference.

Light

Our softest floor.

Engineered to bend almost exactly like Pro, with larger fiberglass tubes on a looser 24 in. span. Fewer tubes make it faster to install, less expensive and taller, and the wider pad spacing lets it give a little more, so it is our softest floor. It is sometimes the right choice for a room with a specific height. Custom heights are possible.

Kit

Cost-effective and long lasting.

A single layer floor designed for budget-conscious customers. It matches the force reduction of a basket weave floor, with less deflection. FiberSprung technicians can build it, or your general contractor can, and every Kit comes with design services from FiberSprung. It uses the same materials as our other floors, so it has the same long life.

Go

Portable.

A single layer travel floor in 4 ft by 4 ft panels that lock to each other mechanically. One layer of fiberglass is fastened to the underside of each plywood panel. The only tool is an 8 mm hex wrench, which we provide, and any hardware store has one too. A team of four can put down 800 sq ft in two to three hours; a basket weave floor the same size takes about two days.

React Marley.

FiberSprung's own all-purpose composite marley, for ballet, pointe, contemporary, modern, jazz, street, hip-hop, aerobics and pilates.

React gives a consistent surface, so dancers can trust it to feel the same across an entire room or stage. It is a three layer composite: a vinyl performance surface, stabilized by a fiberglass layer for reinforcement and dimensional stability, and backed with closed-cell foam that conforms to the floor below.

Caring for React Marley

  • Keep the tube from the center of the roll. Roll the surface around it and store it standing up, to avoid flat spots and bubbling when it goes down again.
  • Clean with a non-abrasive broom or a vacuum. Mop with water and a vinyl marley cleaning solution.
  • Never use bleach, acetone, ammonia, rosin, detergent or strong vinegar.
