Why fiberglass.

Pine varies with its fibers and its water content. In the same bend test, our pultruded fiberglass tube bent 3 mm every time. Every part of a FiberSprung floor follows from that.

Four steps.

From the drawing to the surface you dance on, with the same steps in a real room.

  1. 1Design and planning

    Every component is made from 3D CAD, with tolerances on the drawings. Your floor is laid out in 2D CAD from architectural CAD, measurements or scans.

  2. 2The grid

    Pultruded fiberglass tubes in a basket weave, joined with elastomer fittings and aluminum joint brackets.

    Use life
    30+ years
    Warranty
    20 years

  3. 3Plywood support surface

    Plywood coated with a water-based outdoor sealant and fastened to the grid at the perimeter.

    Plywood
    3/4 in. or 1 in.
    Warranty
    2 years

  4. 4Performance surface

    Choose the surface for the work: dance marley, engineered hardwood or vinyl click-lock. FiberSprung has its own React Marley and is a dealer for other marley.

    Surface installation is not included in floor pricing.
