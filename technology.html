Why fiberglass.

Pine varies with its fibers and its water content. In the same bend test, our pultruded fiberglass tube bent 3 mm every time. Every part of a FiberSprung floor follows from that.

The bend test Materials The four steps Repair and moving

Same bend,
every time.

Our fiberglass was purpose-designed to match the bend of a classic wooden sprung floor, with much more consistency. To show it, we bent 1x4 pine and our fiberglass tube over a 4 ft span, with 50 lb at the center. Pine moved 3 to 7 mm, and some pieces bent unevenly. Our tube moved 3 mm every time.

Pine modulus of elasticity
6,800 to 12,100 MPa, depending on fibers and water content

Four steps.

  1. 1Design and planning

    To quote your floor, we lay it out roughly in CAD from architectural drawings, measurements or scans. Once you order and the deposit is paid, we make a detailed layout and a bill of materials. Planned layouts mean less waste and lower cost, and they give the install team its plan.

  2. 2The grid

    Pultruded fiberglass tubes in a basket weave, joined with elastomer fittings, aluminum joint brackets and steel fasteners. After you order, the lead time is about 6 weeks, and the fiberglass parts arrive pre-assembled with FiberSprung fittings.

    Use life
    30+ years
    Warranty
    20 years

  3. 3Plywood support surface

    Plywood coated with a water-based outdoor sealant and fastened to the grid at the perimeter.

    Warranty
    2 years

  4. 4Performance surface

    Choose the surface for the work: dance marley, engineered hardwood or vinyl click-lock. FiberSprung has its own React Marley and is a dealer for other marley.

    Surface installation is not included in floor pricing.

Built to be
taken apart.

A FiberSprung floor is never permanently installed in a studio. It is an asset.

The surface is removable and the parts are interchangeable, so a floor can be opened, repaired, moved or sold. When life changes, we help customers relocate their floors or give them a new life with someone else.

Foam can never be patched to match, because new foam is not the same age as the rest of the floor. Wood takes a lot of time and effort to take apart and rebuild.
