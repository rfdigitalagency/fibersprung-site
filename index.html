Who uses it

The future of
sprung flooring.

Sprung floors prevent injuries and promote joint health. They absorb shock, reducing pain and fatigue. A sprung floor gets its spring from what is underneath. We build that part from pultruded fiberglass instead of wood or foam, so it bends the same way every time, resists water, and can be repaired, moved or resold.

Our fiberglass is designed to bend, and it works far below its failure load, so it keeps its spring year after year. A wood sprung floor loses too much spring in about 15 years, and foam starts losing its spring from the day it goes down.

Fiberglass,
wood
or foam.

Wood and foam floors are installed for good. They become part of the building, cannot move with you, and are thrown out at the end of their life. Wood also reacts to water, temperature and humidity.

How often do you replace your kitchen sponge, or your sneakers? Force reduction testing shows that foam takes about a minute, unloaded, to fully rebound. Is that floor actually sprung, or does it just squish?

It started with
a sprinkler.

The idea for FiberSprung came when Spencer Hering lost a wood sprung dance floor to a sprinkler system malfunction. Then, in 2017, Hurricanes Harvey, Irma and Maria destroyed sprung floors across Texas, Florida and Puerto Rico. Spencer had built dance floors before, and got to thinking there had to be a better way.

On his final day as a professional dancer, he brought the idea to product designer Micah Reyes, whose background is composites design for aerospace and yachts. Together they saw what fiberglass could do for a sprung floor: a longer life, more consistency and a better floor.

Moveable, repairable,
resellable.

A FiberSprung floor is never permanently installed in a studio. It is an asset.

Our floors are designed to be taken apart and moved without losing any of their spring. When life changes, we help customers relocate their floors, or resell them.

Damage happens in studios sometimes, and it is no one's fault. The surface lifts in minutes, so a repair costs little. We are your flooring partner for life.
