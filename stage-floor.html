Stage and Stage Pro

Stage is a single layer floor: one layer of fiberglass tubes under the surface. Stage Pro is a basket weave, which spreads each jump over more pads for a more consistent feel. Both are fully sprung and built for high loads.

Stage

High load, cost effective, fully sprung stage.

An all-purpose stage for facilities that need a fully sprung stage for periodic dance performances, without the performance of Stage Pro. Clear your storage of your portable sprung floor, and have one floor for every purpose.
