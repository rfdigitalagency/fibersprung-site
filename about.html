About
FiberSprung.

The people behind FiberSprung, and the Rhode Island shop where the floors are built.

It started with
a sprinkler.

The idea for FiberSprung came when Spencer Hering lost a wood sprung dance floor to a sprinkler system malfunction. Then, in the summer of 2017, Hurricanes Harvey, Irma and Maria destroyed sprung floors across Texas, Florida and Puerto Rico, showing the need for a sprung floor that could survive water. Spencer had built dance floors in the past, and got to thinking there had to be a better way.

On his final day as a professional dancer, Spencer brought the idea to product designer Micah Reyes. Micah has a background in composites design through aerospace and yacht design. They quickly saw the potential of composites such as fiberglass to increase the life, consistency and quality of sprung floors, and the research and development for FiberSprung began.

Today FiberSprung uses high performance, long lasting composites like fiberglass in place of the wood and foam inside sprung floors for dance and martial arts.
