About
FiberSprung.

The people behind FiberSprung, and the Rhode Island shop where the floors are built.

It started with
a sprinkler.

The idea for FiberSprung began when Spencer Hering's mother lost a wood sprung dance floor to a sprinkler malfunction. Spencer had built dance floors before, and thought there had to be a better way.

On his last day as a professional dancer, his friend Micah Reyes was visiting. Micah's background is sailing composites design, and he saw what composites such as fiberglass could do for a sprung floor: more consistency, and a much longer life.

FiberSprung is the better way they built.
